# INC-012 — Codeshare segments crashing the itinerary view

**Date:** 2026-03-22
**Duration:** 2h 45m
**Severity:** SEV-2
**Author:** Itinerary team

## Summary

The itinerary page crashed for any booking containing a codeshare segment,
immediately after a partner deployed a change to their schedule API.

## Timeline

- **13:00** - Partner deploys. No notice to us; the change was not breaking by
  their versioning policy.
- **13:04** - Client-side error rate on the itinerary page spikes.
- **15:45** - Defensive access shipped.

## Impact

Every customer with a codeshare booking, roughly 8% of active itineraries, saw
a blank page with a generic error for nearly three hours.

## What went wrong

The itinerary renderer reads the operating carrier through a chain of nested
property accesses on the partner's schedule response. The partner moved the
carrier object one level deeper and kept the old field as a deprecated alias
that returns null.

Nothing validates the shape of the response at the boundary. The nested access
therefore threw on a null intermediate, and the exception propagated up through
the renderer.

We had worked before the deploy and after it we had not, with no change on our
side. The response shape was an assumption held in our code and nowhere
written down or enforced.

## Resolution

The partner response is now parsed through a declared schema at the boundary,
with a clear error when it does not match.

## Action items

- Parse external responses at the boundary rather than reading them deep in the
  render path.
- Ask the partner for a change feed we can subscribe to.
